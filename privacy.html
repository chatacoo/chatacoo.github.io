Privacy policy

beelink mail archive is a personal program written and used by one person, its owner, to keep an archive of the owner's own email. Nobody else uses it.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent, it reads the owner's own Gmail mailbox using Google's read-only permission (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, modify, or delete mail. It requests no other Google data.

Where the data goes

Messages are copied to an encrypted disk on a computer the owner physically controls. They are not sent to, stored with, or shared with any third party, and they are not used for advertising, analytics, or training of any kind.

Who can see it

Only the owner. There are no other users, no accounts, and no access for anyone else.

Retention and deletion

The owner keeps the archive for as long as they choose and can delete it at any time. Access can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account under Security → Third-party apps & services, which immediately stops any further reading.

Contact

The owner's contact address is shown on the Google consent screen for this application.
